Weekly progression

Week 1RPE 5–6

Focus on movement quality.

Week 2Load ↑

Slight increase in load, or 1–2 extra reps where appropriate.

Week 3RPE 6–7

Add a little volume — only if recovery is good.

Week 4RPE 7

Finish strong, but never reach failure.

RPE — rate of perceived exertion on a 1–10 scale. RPE 7 means roughly three good reps left in reserve. Keep every set short of failure across all four weeks.
